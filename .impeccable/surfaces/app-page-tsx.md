## Direction contract

THESIS: A small business's tech is one continuous relationship: start, grow, support. The homepage owns that as a single vertical timeline, each stage proven by a real client site. It refuses the agency default of a hero mock-up, a feature grid, a logo strip, testimonials and a pricing table.

OWN-WORLD: "Tightened sage" (v7). Forest, pine and moss greens on chalk and paper grounds, with hairline rules in linen. One accent beat per screen: claret on light grounds, ochre on forest. Newsreader display at weight 500 (one italic word per composition), Geist body, Geist Mono for markers, status, prices and nav. 2px corners, pills only on buttons, no gradients or glows, shadows at 12% or less. Lists use a mono plus sign, never ticks. Services carry a "Live" or "Ask us" tag.

STORY: The visitor understands that Dorza takes care of the tech so they can run the business. They believe it because each stage shows a real, live client build and only promises services that are live. They act by pressing "Get a quote" and expect a plan and a price within 72 hours.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On chalk, a two-line Newsreader headline at display-lg sets down line by line: "You run the business. / We'll mind the tech.", with "mind" in claret italic, the only claret on screen. Under it, a single lede, the primary pine pill "Get a quote" with a quiet "See the work" text link beside it, and a mono line, "Quote within 72 hours · No lock-in". At 1280 by 800, the top of the timeline spine, its first tick and the marker "DAY ONE" are visible above the fold.

FORM: Editorial timeline (a single spine at a quarter of the width, three stages, a case-study card per stage where one exists). First on the ordered list. seed: none (pinned)

Signature interaction: as the journey section scrolls through view, a pine line draws down the hairline spine and each stage's tick crosses it. With reduced motion the line is fully drawn and nothing moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
